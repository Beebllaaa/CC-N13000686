About this work

Play this audiovisual instrument with your hands and face. Click the canvas and allow camera access to start the background beat.

Turn tracks on or off. Touch your left thumb to one of your other left fingertips. From index finger to pinky, the tracks are bass, keys, plucked strings, and percussion. Release and pinch again to turn the track off.

Adjust a track's volume. Use your right thumb and index finger to pinch a track marker. Pull it outward to turn the volume up, or push it back toward the left fingertip to turn it down.

Tap out a rhythm. Touch your left thumb with your right index finger to play a short percussion hit. Lift your finger before tapping again.

Adjust the overall volume. Open your mouth wider to make the whole mix louder. Close it to turn the volume down.

Change the tempo. Blink at least four times within three seconds to speed up the beat and all four tracks together. Faster playback also raises the pitch. When you stop blinking, the music returns to its original tempo and pitch.

Reset the tracks. Hold a left-hand fist briefly to turn off all four tracks and reset their volume levels. The background beat keeps playing.
